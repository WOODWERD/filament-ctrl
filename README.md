# Filament Ctrl

Keep count of the filament on your shelf and know which spools are in the printer. Filament Ctrl also holds Bambu Lab's material catalog, every line and colour, with a short guide to choosing a material.

Your own Claude, with the WOODWERD filament skill in [`skill/`](skill/), reads your filament orders and Bambu Studio's files, and writes proposals you review in the app. Your record lives in your own private GitHub repo. Nothing is sent to WOODWERD.

**Open it:** [woodwerd.github.io/filament-ctrl](https://woodwerd.github.io/filament-ctrl/). **How to use it:** the [user guide](https://woodwerd.github.io/filament-ctrl/guide.html).
